**Document ID:** `docs/battery_cell_selection.md`

**Decision Context:** `docs/plan1.md` Decision **D16** (`Option B`): two identical 1S Li-polymer pouch cells in series (2S, 8.40 V max) co-located in the **left temple** with an **HY2120** 2S protector + dual N-FET (`references_local/SCH-4a_preflight/HY2120.pdf`) and an **Injoinic IP2326_NPD** 2S boost charger (`references_local/SCH-4a_preflight/IP2326_NPD.pdf`, 0.30 A charge, 8.40 V CV, integrated 2S balance).

This audit evaluates real candidates against Requirements 1–7 using downloaded datasheets stored in `docs/datasheets/`.

### 4.35 V HV Cell Compatibility with IP2326_NPD + HY2120
- **Charger (`references_local/SCH-4a_preflight/IP2326_NPD.pdf` pp. 4–5 §§7–8):** `IP2326_NPD` (the variant supporting `< 700 mA` charge current down to `0.30 A`) regulates 2S constant-voltage (`VTRGT`) at **8.3–8.5 V (typ 8.40 V, i.e. 4.20 V/cell)** when `RVSET = NC`, and can only step *down* (`8.3 / 8.2 / 8.1 V`). Only a separate SKU (`IP2326_8V8`, p. 5) raises CV by `+0.4 V` to `8.80 V` (`4.40 V/cell`), which would **overcharge** a `4.35 V` cell (`> 4.35 V`).
- **Protector (`references_local/SCH-4a_preflight/HY2120.pdf` p. 6 §6):** HY2120 overcharge detection (`VCUn`) depends on suffix: `-CB/-DB/-EB/-GB/-NB` trip at `4.28 ± 0.025 V` and `-LB/-OB` at `4.25 ± 0.025 V` (compatible with 4.20 V/cell), whereas `-AB/-BB` trip at `4.35 ± 0.025 V` and `-HB` at `4.38 ± 0.025 V`.
- **Verdict on 4.35 V LiHV cells:** Charging a `3.8 V / 4.35 V` LiHV cell at `8.40 V` (`4.20 V/cell`) with `IP2326_NPD` + `HY2120-CB` is **electrically safe**, but under-charges the cell (sacrificing ~15–20% of rated capacity), and none of the candidate 300 mAh FPV LiHV packs provide a manufacturer datasheet to quantify 4.20 V discharge curves or IR. **All 4.35 V LiHV candidates fail Requirement 1/2 verification.**

## 3. Requirement 2 Verdict & Engineering Tradeoff Options

**Plain Verdict:** **NO candidate passes all 7 requirements simultaneously.** Specifically:
1. **Physics vs. Volume (`≤ 6.0 × ≤ 12.5 × ≤ 40.0 mm`):** That envelope gives `≤ 3.0 cm³` gross volume (~`2.0 cm³` active electrode stack). Standard-rate LiPo cells fitting inside it (`Fullwat LP601235`, `DNK501240`) only deliver **150–190 mAh** and **0.15–0.36 A continuous**. Every real `250–480 mAh` high-rate (`10C–20C`) cell with a verified datasheet is at least **20.5–30.5 mm wide**, and every `11.5–12.0 mm` wide `300 mAh` FPV cell is **50–68 mm long** and **4.35 V LiHV** without a datasheet.
2. **Requirement 2 Pass Status:** Only **`EEMB LP453048HC`** (`480 mAh`, `20C5A = 9.60 A continuous`, `docs/datasheets/EEMB_LP453048HC.pdf` p. 3 §2.8) guarantees `≥ 3.25 A` continuous discharge on a verified datasheet, and it exceeds Req 3 (`480 > 450 mAh`), Req 4 (`30.5 × 49 mm`, `~9.6 g`), and Req 7 (B2B RFQ). **`EEMB LP453030HA`** clears `≥ 2.0 A` continuous (`3.00 A`, `10C`), falls `0.25 A` short of `3.25 A`, and lacks a `≥ 3.25 A for ≥ 10 s` pulse specification.

### Option (i): Higher-C Cell Even If Bigger / Heavier
- **Option (i-A) — `EEMB LP453030HA` (300 mAh, 10C = 3.00 A cont):** `≤ 4.8 × 30.5 × 31 mm`, `~6.0 g` (`12.0 g` pair — saves `4.0 g` vs BOM 16 g). Fits `80 mm` length (`2 × 32 mm = 64 mm` end-to-end), **requires widening left-temple pocket from `12.5 mm` to `≥ 31.5 mm`** (or tilting/tall temple profile `~31 mm` tall × `~6 mm` thick).
- **Option (i-B) — `EEMB LP453048HC` (480 mAh, 20C = 9.60 A cont):** `≤ 4.8 × 30.5 × 49 mm`, `~9.6 g` (`19.2 g` pair — `+3.2 g` over BOM 16 g, still within 120 g D13 if frame is tuned). Exceeds `80 mm` end-to-end (`2 × 50 mm = 100 mm`) unless mounted side-by-side (`9.6 mm` total stack thickness) or split across temples.
- **Option (i-C) — `GNB3001S60WHV` (300 mAh, 60C claim, LiHV):** `6.7 × 11.5 × 51 mm`, `7.0–7.2 g` (`14.4 g` pair). Fits `12.5 mm` width, requires **`~105 mm` temple length** and **`≥ 7.0 mm` cavity height**, **lacks a datasheet**, and runs at reduced capacity when charged to `4.20 V`.

### Option (ii): Software Peak-Power Cap (Max Pack Current & System Watts at `6.0 V`, `90%` Buck Efficiency)
Formula: $P_{\text{sys,max}} = I_{\text{pack,max}} \times 6.0\text{ V} \times 0.90 = 5.4 \times I_{\text{pack,max}}\text{ W}$ (for comparison, at `7.4 V` nominal: $6.66 \times I_{\text{pack,max}}\text{ W}$).

### Option (iii): Bigger Capacity
Increasing capacity to **`480–500 mAh`** (e.g., `EEMB LP453048HC` at `480 mAh`, `20C = 9.60 A`, or a `500 mAh` `10C` cell = `5.00 A` continuous) reduces the C-rate needed for `3.25 A` from `10.8C` down to **`6.5C–6.8C`**, keeps IR lower (`≤ 100 mΩ` on `LP453048HC`, cutting $I^2 R$ drop at `3.25 A` from `~1.04 V` across two `160 mΩ` cells to `~0.65 V`), and boosts active duty runtime by `~60%`, at the cost of `~19.2 g` pack mass (`+3.2 g` vs BOM) and larger housing volume.

## 4. Recommendation & Fuse / PTC Coordination

### Primary Recommendation
1. **Recommended 300 mAh Cell (Electrical + Weight Match): `EEMB LP453030HA`** (`docs/datasheets/EEMB_LP453030HA.pdf`), paired with **Option (ii) a software peak-power cap of `≤ 16.0 W` (`≤ 2.96 A` at `6.0 V`, `90%` buck)** and **left-temple cavity redesign to `31.5 mm` height/width** (or vertical stacking).
   - **Why:** It is the lightest (`~6.0 g/cell`, `12.0 g` pair) bare 1S `3.7 V / 4.20 V` cell with a complete 9-page manufacturer datasheet verifying `300 mAh` (`270 mAh` min), `1C` (`0.30 A`) charge acceptance matching `IP2326_NPD`, `0–45 °C` / `-20–60 °C` operating range, `≤ 160 mΩ` IR, no conflicting internal PCM, and `3.00 A` (`10C`) continuous discharge (`16.20 W` @ `6.0 V`, `90%` buck — within `0.25 A` of `3.25 A`).

### Fuse / PTC Rating Calculation (`references_local/SCH-4a_preflight/Littelfuse-451-453.pdf` p. 2)
The pack fuse must satisfy: $I_{\text{load,peak}} < I_{\text{fuse,rated}} < I_{\text{cell,max}}$ so normal peak bursts never cause nuisance trips while faults open before damaging the cells.

- **With `EEMB LP453030HA` (`I_cell,max = 3.00 A`):** Since `3.00 A < 3.25 A` uncapped peak load, **no standard fuse rating exists strictly between `3.25 A` and `3.00 A`**.
  - Enforcing **Option (ii)** software power cap at **`≤ 2.50 A`** (`13.5 W` @ `6.0 V / 90%`) or **`≤ 2.95 A`** (`15.9 W`) allows a **Littelfuse Nano² `0451 003` (`3.0 A`)** very fast-acting SMF fuse (p. 2: holds `100% = 3.0 A` for `≥ 4 hours`; opens at `200% = 6.0 A` in `≤ 5 s`; cold $R = 22.7\text{ m}\Omega$, voltage drop `68 mV` @ `3.0 A`). Fast overcurrent faults (`3.5–5 A`) must be caught by **HY2120** (`VDIP = 200 mV` across dual N-FETs in ~12 ms), leaving `0451 003` as the catastrophic last-resort FET-short fuse.
- **With `EEMB LP453048HC` (`I_cell,max = 9.60 A`):** A **Littelfuse Nano² `0451 004` (`4.0 A`)** (or `0451 03.5` `3.5 A`) fits **cleanly between `3.25 A` peak load and `9.60 A` cell max**:
  - `0451 004` (`4.0 A`, p. 2): holds `100% = 4.0 A` for `≥ 4 hours` (safe margin above `3.25 A` bursts), opens at `200% = 8.0 A` in `≤ 5 s` (**strictly `< 9.60 A` cell limit**), cold $R = 16.0\text{ m}\Omega$ (`52 mV` drop @ `3.25 A`).
- *(A PTC resettable fuse is NOT recommended here due to higher series resistance and wide thermal hold/trip derating on a warm temple frame; use a Nano² `451/453` fast SMF fuse + HY2120 resettable FET protection.)*

#### Fuse Selection (`references_local/SCH-4a_preflight/Littelfuse-451-453.pdf` p. 2)
Under **D17**, worst-case pack load is **`≤ 1.50 A`** (`8 W / 6.0 V / 90% = 1.48 A`). Using Littelfuse Nano² `451/453` Very Fast-Acting SMF (`p. 2`: `100%` hold `≥ 4 hours`, `200%` open `≤ 5 s` for `1/16–10 A`):
- **Preferred for `≥ 2.5–4.0 A` continuous cell (e.g., if `GRP5811047-S2A` `4.0 A` or a bench-qualified `≥ 3 A` FPV cell is verified): `0451 002.` (`2.0 A`):**
  - Holds `100% = 2.0 A` for `≥ 4 h` (`+35%` margin above `1.48 A` under warm-temple derating), clears `200% = 4.0 A` in `≤ 5 s`, cold resistance **`36.7 mΩ`** (`55 mV` drop @ `1.5 A`). (`0451 02.5` `2.5 A` is valid only if cell continuous limit `≥ 3.3–4.0 A` since `200% = 5.0 A ≤ 5 s`.)
- **If continuous cell rating is `2.0 A` (e.g., `GRP5212050-E2A`): `0451 01.6` (`1.6 A`):**
  - Strictly between `1.50 A` load and `2.00 A` cell limit (`200% = 3.20 A ≤ 5 s`, cold $R = 58.0\text{ m}\Omega$, `87 mV` drop @ `1.5 A`), though tight (`8%` thermal margin above `1.48 A`).
- Fast short/overcurrent transients (`~3–5 A`) continue to be tripped in milliseconds by the `HY2120` + dual `AON5816` MOSFET protector, leaving the `0451` SMF fuse as catastrophic FET-short backup.

#### `HY2120` Variant Selection for `4.20 V` Charge (`references_local/SCH-4a_preflight/HY2120.pdf` p. 6 §6)
`IP2326_NPD` (`RVSET = NC`) regulates 2S CV target to **`VTRGT = 8.40 V` typ (`8.3–8.5 V`, i.e., `4.15–4.25 V/cell`)** (`IP2326_NPD.pdf` p. 4):
- **Reject `HY2120-LB`** (`VCUn = 4.25 ± 0.025 V` = `4.225–4.275 V`, p. 6): upper charger tolerance (`4.25 V/cell`) can falsely trigger OVP during normal CV termination (`4.225 V` lower bound).
- **Recommended: `HY2120-CB` or `HY2120-NB`** (p. 6 §6 Model List):
  - **`VCUn = 4.28 ± 0.025 V` (`4.255–4.305 V`), release `VCRn = 4.08 ± 0.05 V`:** Guarantees zero false OVP trips up to `4.25 V/cell` while still arresting overcharge `< 4.305 V`. Compatible with **both** `4.20 V` native LiPo and `4.35 V` LiHV cells charged to `4.20 V`.
  - Overdischarge detection: **`HY2120-CB`** cuts off at `VDLn = 2.90 ± 0.08 V` (release `3.00 ± 0.1 V`), **`HY2120-NB`** cuts off at `VDLn = 2.80 ± 0.08 V` (release `3.00 ± 0.1 V`, better margin against transient pulse sag above typical `2.75 V` cell cutoffs). Both use `VDIP = 200 ± 30 mV`, `VCIP = -210 ± 30 mV`, Code `A` (p. 6).
