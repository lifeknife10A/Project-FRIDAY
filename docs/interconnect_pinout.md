## 2. Signals and end orientation

- **Right–bridge.** CSI0 uses two D-PHY data pairs and one clock pair from the sensor to the SoC. The CSV maps each polarity to the RK3576 ball and DF37 contact. I2C4_M3 runs at 1.8 V. The bridge receives a 3.3 V source for local 1.1 V and 2.8 V regulation and a 1.8 V feed for I/O and the mics. Direct vs. locally sequenced 1.8 V camera I/O is **UNVERIFIED**. An enable goes to the bridge in this option. The bridge generates MCLK and POR-controlled XCLR, with host MCLK, host reset and camera-cradle NTC reserved. Confirm AF supply and control on the factory assembly. Pi J2 defines no separate host AF wire. The mic return data goes to SAI1 SDI, with SAI1 driving both clocks. - **Right–left auxiliary.** SAI1 BCLK/LRCK/SDO and SD_MODE reach the left amp. The switched 5 V amp feed and the right USB-C VBUS charge feed are separate. DOCK_DET uses GPIO4_D0 (1AD2), CHG_STAT uses GPIO4_D1 (AK4), and CHG_EN uses GPIO0_A7 (1U21) as open drain. Pull-up domains are VCCIO7 for the inputs and PMUIO0 for control. CHG_STAT is inverted IP2326 BAT_STAT, which indicates trickle versus later charge stages rather than charge complete. ## 3. Current, voltage and IR-drop screen

D17 caps the 5 V bus burst at 8 W, corresponding to 1.48 A at 6.0 V and assumed 90% buck efficiency. The pack and harness screen is 1.5 A continuous and 3 A pulse. Cell qualification and permitted pulse duration are open. The 2.0 A fuse is a backup and the low-side BMS provides the fast protection. Bench-check fuse pulse survival, hot derating and BMS threshold together. The charger target is 0.20 A at 8.4 V nominal. [D16 and D17](plan1.md#L317) · [Battery cell selection, round 2](battery_cell_selection.md#L107)

Hirose lists 0.5 A DC and 50 mΩ maximum contact resistance for both FH12 models. With 50% derating use **0.25 A/contact maximum** for screening, or less when the selected flex or thermal limits require it. Size forward and return separately with ceil(I / 0.25 A). For IR drop use R = ρL/(width × thickness), with **UNVERIFIED** model inputs of 0.01724 Ω·mm²/m copper resistivity, 0.25 mm copper width and 35 µm copper. Add 50 mΩ per connector end per conductor. At upper estimated lengths the resulting single-path resistance is about 0.228 Ω right–bridge and 0.356 Ω auxiliary. The loop examples assume equal contact sharing. Inrush, heat and real return sharing remain **UNVERIFIED**. ## 4. High-speed and connector check

**CSI-2.** HDG Table 3-30, PDF p. 243 (printed p. 224), gives 100 Ω ±10% differential, with 95 Ω ±10% fallback when stack-up limits apply, <6 mil intra-pair skew, <12 mil data-to-clock match, <6 in maximum length and four vias maximum. It is under the D/C PHY TX heading. Apply these as conservative channel targets for CSI RX and validate end to end with actual lane rate, sensor tail, bridge filters and connectors. Keep continuous return reference and use the ground contacts before, between and after pairs. Budget the recommended 4× trace-width spacing, with 3× minimum per the table. Keep battery current in the independent harness. FH12 mechanical compatibility and pitch give no guarantee of 100 Ω through standard FFC. The standard FFC channel is **UNVERIFIED**. Provision a controlled-impedance custom flex, check impedance with the chosen supplier and validate the folded channel. [HDG PDF p. 243](datasheets/Rockchip%20RK3576%20Hardware%20Design%20Guide.pdf#page=243) · [Audit §§C.3–C.4](hardware_gap_audit.md#L141)

**I2S/I2C and USB.** Reference BCLK/LRCK and data to the adjacent ground plane, check source damping and branch loading, and verify clock/data timing at the left amp and front mics. Allowable cable lengths and electrical timing are **UNVERIFIED**. On 1.8 V camera I2C, verify cable capacitance, effective pull-up value and rise time at the selected bus speed. Keep camera I2C distinct from PMIC I2C. USB data is local to right J1, and the inter-temple VBUS branch carries power. If USB data moves, HDG Table 3-28, PDF p. 242 (printed p. 223), specifies 90 Ω ±10%, <20 mil intra-pair skew, <6 in carrier trace, fewer than four preferred vias and six maximum.
