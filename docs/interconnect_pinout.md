The older plan routes audio and battery conductors through the bridge PCB. This v0 layout option runs them continuously past the camera carrier, with independent terminations in the temples. ## 3. Current, voltage and IR-drop screen

The project estimates a 16.5 W transient and 3.2–3.5 A on the 5 V system bus. At the specified screening inputs of 6.0 V depleted pack and 90% buck efficiency, 16.5 W gives 3.06 A pack current. Using the upper bus current gives 17.5 W, or 3.24 A at the battery. Round up to **3.25 A UNVERIFIED** for harness screening. The BOM's lower operating-power estimates cover different duty points. A ~3 A BMS trip with a reported 2.5–3.5 A spread remains **UNVERIFIED** and can overlap this load. Validate thresholds, delays and low-battery current limits. The 300 mA charger target and IP2326 programming also require bench confirmation. [Component spec lines 61–83](schematic_components.md#L61) · [BOM lines 148–151 and 241–252](hardware_bom.md#L148) · [Audit §E](hardware_gap_audit.md#L281)

Hirose lists 0.5 A DC and 50 mΩ maximum contact resistance for both FH12 models. With 50% derating use **0.25 A/contact maximum** for screening, or less when the selected flex or thermal limits require it. Size forward and return separately with ceil(I / 0.25 A). For IR drop use R = ρL/(width × thickness), with **UNVERIFIED** model inputs of 0.01724 Ω·mm²/m copper resistivity, 0.25 mm copper width and 35 µm copper. Add 50 mΩ per connector end per conductor. At upper estimated lengths the resulting single-path resistance is about 0.228 Ω right–bridge and 0.356 Ω auxiliary. The loop examples assume equal contact sharing. Inrush, heat and real return sharing remain **UNVERIFIED**. ### Split cells — DECISION FOR KRISHNA

With one cell per temple, left cell positive reaches right cell negative through CELL_MID across both hinges. If the right buck taps right cell positive locally, full discharge still crosses both hinges on CELL_MID and again on the protected pack return. PACK_POS takes the right cell positive to the left charging/protection circuit. If high-side protection is placed on the left, a separate raw/protected positive path may be required and the provisional three-lead harness would need revision. Kelvin midpoint sensing must remain high impedance and distinct from the current-carrying midpoint. The BOM's 35 mA bleed and older plan's ~55 mA are different circuit estimates. Actual bleed and fault currents are **UNVERIFIED**. Validate open-sense behavior and ensure auxiliary grounds, shield and USB cannot bypass the BMS return path. ## 4. High-speed and connector check

**CSI-2.** HDG Table 3-30, PDF p. 243 (printed p. 224), gives 100 Ω ±10% differential, with 95 Ω ±10% fallback when stack-up limits apply, <6 mil intra-pair skew, <12 mil data-to-clock match, <6 in maximum length and four vias maximum. It is under the D/C PHY TX heading. Apply these as conservative channel targets for CSI RX and validate end to end with actual lane rate, sensor tail, bridge filters and connectors. Keep continuous return reference and use the ground contacts before, between and after pairs. Budget the recommended 4× trace-width spacing, with 3× minimum per the table. Keep battery current in the independent harness. FH12 mechanical compatibility and pitch give no guarantee of 100 Ω through standard FFC. The standard FFC channel is **UNVERIFIED**. Provision a controlled-impedance custom flex, check impedance with the chosen supplier and validate the folded channel.
